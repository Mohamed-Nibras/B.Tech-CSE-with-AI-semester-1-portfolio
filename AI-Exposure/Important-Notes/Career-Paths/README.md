# 💼 Career Paths

This chapter explores major career paths within technology and Artificial Intelligence.

The objective was to understand what different technology professionals do, the skills they require, the tools they use, and how each role contributes to building modern software and AI systems.

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

### Week 15 — Coding Career Paths

Career Paths Explored:

- Software Engineer (SWE)
- Machine Learning Engineer (MLE)
- Data Scientist (DS)
- AI Engineer

Areas Studied:

- Job responsibilities
- Required skills
- Common tools
- Career differences
- Career overlap
- Industry relevance
- Personal career interests

---

## 🎯 Key Learning

Although different technology careers appear very different on the surface, most of them share a common foundation:

- Programming
- Problem Solving
- Logical Thinking
- Debugging
- Software Engineering Fundamentals

Strong foundations create flexibility across multiple career paths.
