# Week 7 – Startups, Unicorns, Founders & Funding

## Overview

This week focused on understanding how startups are created, funded, validated, and scaled. The goal was to learn how successful technology companies evolve from solving a problem into building large-scale products.

---

## Key Concepts Learned

### What is a Startup?

A startup is a company created to solve a real problem and scale rapidly, often using technology.

Unlike traditional businesses, startups prioritize growth, experimentation, and innovation.

### Startup Lifecycle

Problem → Idea → MVP → Validation → Iteration → Scaling

A startup begins by identifying a real problem, building a simple solution, testing it with users, improving it based on feedback, and eventually scaling.

### Unicorn Startups

A unicorn is a startup valued at over $1 billion.

Examples include:

* OpenAI
* Swiggy
* Razorpay
* Zepto

### Startup Funding

Common funding stages include:

* Seed Funding
* Series A
* Series B
* Series C

Funding helps startups grow rapidly before reaching profitability.

### Founder Mindset

Successful founders often demonstrate:

* Problem obsession
* Fast execution
* User focus
* Long-term thinking
* Ability to handle uncertainty

---

## Startup Patterns Observed

Successful startups generally:

* Solve painful problems
* Start with a simple MVP
* Focus on a specific audience
* Iterate quickly
* Scale only after validation

## Key Takeaways

* Startups solve problems, not just build products.
* MVPs are more important than perfect products.
* Validation is critical before scaling.
* Execution matters more than ideas.
* Technology is only valuable when it creates user value.
