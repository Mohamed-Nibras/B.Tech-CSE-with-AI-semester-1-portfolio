# Week 1 — What is AI? Types & Real-World Use Cases

## 🎯 Objective

To understand the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and their real-world applications.

---

## 🧠 Topics Covered

### 1. Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples:

* Virtual assistants
* Recommendation systems
* Chatbots
* Self-driving vehicles

---

### 2. Machine Learning

Machine Learning is a subset of AI that enables computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions without being explicitly programmed for every scenario.

Examples:

* Spam detection
* Product recommendations
* Fraud detection

---

### 3. Deep Learning

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers.

Applications:

* Image recognition
* Speech recognition
* Language translation
* Generative AI systems

Popular Architectures:

* CNN
* RNN
* Transformer Models

---

### 4. Types of Machine Learning

#### Supervised Learning

Learns from labeled data.

Examples:

* Email spam detection
* House price prediction

---

#### Unsupervised Learning

Finds hidden patterns in unlabeled data.

Examples:

* Customer segmentation
* Data clustering

---

#### Self-Supervised Learning

Generates labels from existing data and learns patterns automatically.

Examples:

* Large Language Models
* Modern AI systems

---

#### Reinforcement Learning

Learns through rewards and penalties.

Examples:

* Game-playing AI
* Robotics

---

### 5. AI Use Cases

#### Prediction

Forecasting future outcomes.

Examples:

* Weather prediction
* Stock analysis

#### Clustering

Grouping similar items.

Examples:

* Customer segmentation

#### Anomaly Detection

Finding unusual behavior.

Examples:

* Fraud detection

#### Computer Vision

Understanding images and videos.

Examples:

* Face recognition
* Object detection

#### Natural Language Understanding

Understanding human language.

Examples:

* ChatGPT
* Voice assistants

#### Voice Analysis

Understanding speech and audio.

Examples:

* Siri
* Alexa

#### Action Optimization

Selecting optimal actions.

Examples:

* Route planning

#### Generative AI

Creating new content.

Examples:

* ChatGPT
* DALL·E

---

### 6. Types of AI

#### Narrow AI

Designed for a specific task.

Examples:

* Chatbots
* Recommendation systems

---

#### General AI (Theoretical)

A system capable of performing any intellectual task that a human can perform.

Currently does not exist.

---

#### Self-Aware AI (Theoretical)

A hypothetical AI with self-consciousness and awareness.

Currently does not exist.

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

* AI is the broad field.
* Machine Learning is a subset of AI.
* Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning.
* Most modern AI systems use data to learn patterns and make decisions.
* AI is already used in many industries and applications.
