# 🚀 Startup Concept: IronPath

## Problem

Beginners often feel overwhelmed by the abundance of learning resources available online.

They struggle with:

- Decision paralysis
- Information overload
- Lack of direction
- Uncertainty about what to learn next
- Fear of wasting time

---

## Solution

IronPath is a decision engine that helps beginners gain clarity and confidence in their learning journey.

Instead of simply generating roadmaps, it provides:

- Personalized direction
- Progress tracking
- Learning guidance
- Adaptive recommendations

The goal is to reduce uncertainty and help users stay consistent.
