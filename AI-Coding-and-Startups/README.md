A structured 17-week exploration of Artificial Intelligence, Coding, Technology, Product Thinking, Startups, Career Paths, and the modern tech ecosystem.

This learning journey was completed as part of my Semester 1 self-development roadmap alongside Python Programming, Mathematics for CS/AI, and Logical Problem Solving.

## 🎯 Objective

The goal of this track was to gain exposure to:

- Artificial Intelligence fundamentals
- Machine Learning concepts
- Modern AI tools and applications
- Startup ecosystems and founder thinking
- Product development principles
- GitHub and technology workflows
- Career opportunities in technology

Rather than focusing on deep technical implementation, this subject focused on building awareness, curiosity, and industry understanding.
