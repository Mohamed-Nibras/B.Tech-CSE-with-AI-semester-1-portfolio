# Week 9 – Product Thinking, MVPs & User Experience

## Overview

This week focused on understanding how successful products are designed around user needs rather than features.

Swiggy was studied as a real-world case study.

---

## Key Concepts Learned

### Product Thinking

A product is not an application.

A product is a solution to a user problem.

Successful products focus on:

* User needs
* Simplicity
* Reliability
* Ease of use

### MVP (Minimum Viable Product)

An MVP is the simplest version of a product that delivers value.

Purpose:

* Test assumptions
* Gather feedback
* Validate demand
* Reduce risk

### User Experience (UX)

Good UX reduces effort.

Good UX removes friction.

Good UX makes actions intuitive.

---

## Swiggy Case Study

### Problem Solved

Before Swiggy:

* Manual restaurant calls
* Uncertain delivery times
* No order tracking
* Limited restaurant discovery

### MVP

Swiggy initially focused on:

* Restaurant discovery
* Order placement
* Reliable delivery

### UX Strengths

* Simple ordering flow
* Clear categories
* Real-time tracking
* Clean interface
* Transparent delivery information

### Friction Removed

* No phone calls
* Reduced uncertainty
* Better restaurant visibility
* Faster ordering experience

## Why Users Love Swiggy

Users value:

## Key Takeaways

* Products solve problems.
* Simplicity beats complexity.
* User trust matters.
* Great UX removes friction.
* MVPs help validate ideas faster.
