# Week 04 — How Google Search Works

## Overview

Google Search operates through three major stages:

1. Crawling
2. Indexing
3. Serving and Ranking

---

# 1. Crawling

## Definition

Crawling is the process of discovering new and updated webpages across the internet.

Google uses automated programs called crawlers.

Main crawler:

- Googlebot

---

## What Googlebot Does

- Visits webpages
- Downloads page content
- Follows links
- Discovers new URLs

---

## Crawling Process

### Step 1: URL Discovery

Google discovers URLs through:

- Existing webpages
- Internal links
- External links
- XML sitemaps

---

### Step 2: Fetching

Google downloads webpage data.

This includes:

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

---

### Step 3: Rendering

Google renders the webpage similarly to a browser.

This helps Google understand:

- Visible content
- Interactive elements
- JavaScript-generated content

---

## Crawling Limitations

Google may avoid:

- Low-quality pages
- Duplicate pages
- Login-protected content
- Blocked URLs

---

# 2. Indexing

## Definition

Indexing is the process of organizing webpage information into Google's database.

---

## Information Analyzed

Google extracts:

- Text content
- Keywords
- Images
- Videos
- Metadata
- Headings

---

## Duplicate Detection

Google groups similar pages.

It selects:

- Canonical version
- Most useful version

for search results.

---

# 3. Serving and Ranking

## Definition

When a user searches something, Google retrieves relevant pages from its index.

---

## Ranking Factors

Google evaluates hundreds of signals.

Important examples:

### Content Quality

- Relevance
- Accuracy
- Helpfulness

### User Context

- Location
- Language
- Device

### Site Quality

- Trustworthiness
- Reputation
- Originality

---

# Anatomy of a Search Result

A search result usually contains:

## Title Link

The clickable headline.

Generated from:

- Page title
- Headings
- Content

---

## Snippet

Short description below the title.

Purpose:

- Preview page content

---

## Attribution Features

Include:

- Site name
- URL
- Favicon

---

# Key Learnings

- Search engines rely on indexing.
- Crawling must happen before indexing.
- Indexing must happen before ranking.
- Search results are not random.
- Quality and relevance influence visibility.
