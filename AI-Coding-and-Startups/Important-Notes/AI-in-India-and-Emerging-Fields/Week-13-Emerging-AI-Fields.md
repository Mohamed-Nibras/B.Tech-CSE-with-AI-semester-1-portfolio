# Week 13 — Emerging AI Fields

## 🎯 Topic

Emerging Fields: Robotics, Vision, Healthcare

## 📌 Objective

Explore the major industries where Artificial Intelligence is being applied and understand how AI creates real-world impact.

---

## 🤖 AI Fields Explored

### Robotics

AI enables robots to:

- Automate repetitive tasks
- Navigate environments
- Make decisions using sensors

Examples:

- Factory robots
- Humanoid robots
- Autonomous systems

---

### Computer Vision

AI analyzes images and videos for:

- Object detection
- Face recognition
- Surveillance
- Visual automation

Examples:

- Face unlock
- Medical imaging
- Traffic sign detection

---

### Healthcare AI

AI assists doctors through:

- Disease detection
- Medical image analysis
- Faster diagnosis

Examples:

- Cancer detection
- Radiology systems
- Diagnostic tools
